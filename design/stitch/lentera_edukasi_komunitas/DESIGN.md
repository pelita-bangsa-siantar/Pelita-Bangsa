## Brand & Style

The brand personality is communal, luminous, empowering, and deeply grassroots. It champions the transformative spirit of literacy across North Sumatra (Pematang Siantar – Simalungun), bridging grassroots community warmth with forward-looking educational leadership. 

The aesthetic is a fusion of **Modern Humanist Minimalism** and **Tactile Optimism**:
- **Tone:** Inviting, cheerful, trustworthy, and organized. It avoids institutional sterility in favor of approachable warmth, honoring children, young scholars, volunteers, and rural community partners.
- **Visual Motif:** Subtle sunbeam angles, warm welcoming entry portals (inspired by the open-door book iconography), and clean editorial layouts that present community literacy projects, catalog collections, and donor milestones with pride and transparency.
- **Mood:** Sunlight cutting through a welcoming library veranda—accessible, hopeful, vibrant, and orderly.

## Colors

The color palette directly extends the emblem's symbolism: the radiant sun of knowledge, the sheltering community roof, and the figures reading together.

- **Primary (`#D97706` / Amber Gold):** Evokes the illuminating lantern ("Pelita") and optimistic curiosity. Used for primary CTAs, active highlights, key metrics, and celebratory banners.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` / Deep Sky Blue):** Represents depth of knowledge, youth, clarity, and structural focus. Serves as interactive indicators, category badges for education/technology, and anchor links.
- **Tertiary (`#E11D48` / Warm Rose-Crimson):** Signals community heartbeat, volunteerism, donation campaigns, urgent alerts, and social action initiatives.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` / Deep Slate Gray):** The grounded foundation borrowed from the roof silhouette. Provides maximum legibility against light backgrounds, anchoring headings and structural borders.
- **Background / Canvas Tones:** Built on clean off-white (`#F8FAFC`) with warm cream accents (`#FEFCE8` for callouts and highlight containers), avoiding cold medical whites in favor of cozy paper-like readability.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, an Indonesian-crafted contemporary geometric typeface that blends geometric precision with soft, warm terminals. 

- **Headlines:** Set with confident weights (`700` and `800`) with snug letter spacing (`-0.02em`) to command attention with dignity and cheerful approachability.
- **Body:** Set with balanced line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensuring stress-free readability for catalog summaries, programmatic updates, and reading circles across varying mobile viewports.
- **Labels & Numbers:** Rendered in semi-bold and bold weights to give metrics, ISBN codes, book tags, and category chips immediate scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system implements a **fluid 12-column grid** anchored by structured safe containers to accommodate both expansive editorial storytelling and dense administrative catalog management:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max container width `1200px`, 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and dynamic margins (`auto`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` screen margins.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` screen margins.

Layouts emphasize vertical breathing room with generous component gaps (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between distinct thematic sections, paired with compact internal card padding (`space-md`) to keep community updates organized and accessible.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient elevation and layered tonal surfaces rather than heavy, harsh shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral light slate surface (`#F8FAFC`) with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shelf Baselines):** Crisp white containers (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border tinted in subtle slate (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient, warm golden-gray diffuse drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Modals):** Lifted cards and action dialogs transition upward with `translate-y(-2px)` and expanded ambient diffusion: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Menus & Drawers):** High-clarity backdrop-filtered frosted glass (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/90`) paired with a bottom hairline border (`#F1F5F9`) and soft low-opacity shadow.

## Shapes

The design system utilizes **Roundedness Level 2 (Rounded)**:
- Standard elements (buttons, inputs, select fields) feature a `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radius.
- Cards, collection containers, and callout panels use `1rem` (`16px`, `rounded-lg`).
- Feature hero containers and spotlight statistics blocks use `1.5rem` (`24px`, `rounded-xl`).
- Category chips, status indicators, and pill badges use full pill geometry (`rounded-full`).

This rounded treatment harmonizes with the soft curves of the logo's reading figures and circular sun, conveying hospitality, safety, and community friendliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid amber (`#D97706`) with white text, font-weight `600`, `0.5rem` radius, subtle inner warmth on hover (`#B45309`).
- **Secondary:** Energetic sky blue outline (`2px solid #0284C7`) or soft tinted fill (`#E0F2FE` with `#0369A1` text).
- **Volunteer / Impact (Action):** Vibrant crimson rose (`#E11D48`) for direct donation and volunteer signups.

### Cards
- **Book & Literacy Cards:** Vertical aspect ratio, `rounded-lg`, subtle slate border, housing high-contrast cover art, category badges at the top-left, availability status pills, and prominent title typography.
- **Community News & Activity Cards:** Horizontal split or stacked editorial cards with author/date meta, brief excerpt in `body-sm`, and warm amber text link cues.
- **Metric / Statistics Cards:** Crisp white or light amber-tinted background (`#FEF3C7`), featuring large tabular numbers (`headline-xl`) in `#D97706` or `#0284C7` paired with concise `label-md` descriptors.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, `label-sm` uppercase.
- Color combinations reflect status:
  - *Sains & Teknologi:* Sky blue tint (`bg-sky-50 text-sky-700`).
  - *Literasi Anak & Dongeng:* Amber tint (`bg-amber-50 text-amber-800`).
  - *Kegiatan & Donasi:* Rose tint (`bg-rose-50 text-rose-700`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** `1px` solid border (`#CBD5E1`), background white, focus ring `2px` solid `#0284C7` with a matching `4px` glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved check targets with `#D97706` active fills and clean white iconography.

### Administrative & Inventory Tables
- Clean zebra rows alternating between white and slate off-canvas tint (`#F8FAFC`), sticky header with `label-md` uppercase typography, and clear action button clusters (edit, view, status toggle).
